These are individual process percentiles, not the five-process medians used
in the older performance reports. Neither version meets the older absolute
targets on this system. The immediate previous version was measured on the
same machine and toolchain to distinguish an existing baseline limitation
from a feature regression; the final candidate shows comparable or lower
latency. This report does not revise the targets or certify them as passing.
The requested point release retains that limitation. Clean M1/8 GB baseline
hardware and the older macOS/Xcode environment were not available here.

Turning on the gutter exposed costly repeated line-identity searches and
font measurements. The candidate caches line numbers and gutter width and
reuses drawing attributes. Numeric edits skip variable-completion previews.
