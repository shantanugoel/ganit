# Currency data sources

## European Central Bank euro foreign exchange reference rates

- Source: ECB statistics
- Feed: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml
- About: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html
- Terms: ESCB statistics may be reused free of charge, for commercial and
  non-commercial purposes, provided the source is quoted and the statistics
  and metadata are not modified
  (https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_statistics/governance_and_quality_framework/html/usage_policy.en.html).
- Notice: The reference rates are published for information purposes only.
  Using them for transaction purposes is strongly discouraged. Ganit stores the
  downloaded rates unmodified. Rates between two non-euro currencies are
  calculated by Ganit from ECB reference rates and are not ECB statistics.
  ECB endorsement is not implied.
